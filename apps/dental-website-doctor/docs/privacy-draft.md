# Draft privacy policy

**Draft, not a published legal policy.** Publisher: root.site. Manifest
support/privacy/terms URLs are planned and require approved working pages.

V0 accepts public practice and Google Maps business URLs. It requests no patient
records, health history, names, emails, passwords or lead information. Do not
provide patient-specific URLs. Public website/business facts are processed only
for the requested assessment; these are not private account records.

When enabled, Google receives the public URL for PageSpeed and business references
for Places. Websites receive anonymous bounded requests. Chromium processes
public scripts in an isolated fresh context. No patient forms are submitted.
No separate model API is called by this deterministic server; ChatGPT receives
the requested structured result as part of the plugin operation.

Local reports/status live in process memory up to 30 minutes, then are deleted;
process exit deletes them sooner. A local job can be explicitly deleted. Downloads
and CLI files are user-controlled and persist until deleted by the user. Samples
are fictional/simulated. Raw chat histories, keys and patient details are not
logged by this application.

Before release, update this draft with the actual hosting/gateway access logs,
recipients, retention, safeguards, support/deletion process and applicable terms.
Review Google data storage/display policies and any future OAuth changes.
